## 1. Edit first

Take everything out. Keep what you wear, fits and feels like you. Move the rest out before you buy a single bin or hanger.

## 2. One in, one out

A closet only stays edited if something leaves when something arrives. When you bring in a new piece, choose one to donate or sell.

## 3. Swap twice a year

Off-season clothes go high and back. Everyday clothes go at arm's reach. Doing this twice a year takes under an afternoon and keeps the closet fitted to the season.

## Zones, not piles

Group by category (tops, trousers, dresses, outerwear), then by color within each. A closet you can read at a glance is a closet you'll keep tidy.
